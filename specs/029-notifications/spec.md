## Assumptions

Correct these now, or the work goes ahead on them.

- **The bell sits at the bottom of the sidebar, not the top** (the user's change to the design, Q1). It goes in the footer row, after the person and Claude's state, as a ghost `IconButton` with a red count. The panel opens upward from it, beside the sidebar, its bottom edge level with the bell's. On the collapsed rail the bell sits above the avatar. Everything else about the panel is as drawn.
- **Every word says Orynx**, never DevHub (CLAUDE.md). "DevHub runs on your computer…" becomes "Orynx runs on your computer…", and so on.
- **Colours are this repo's tokens, not the design's.** The design's `tokens.css` still has the blue accent; `main` has the ink accent (the user's call, 18 Oct 2026). The unread dot, a switch that is on and the volume slider take `--accent` as it is here. The red count is `--danger` / `--on-danger`, as drawn.
- **What Orynx tells you about, in this spec** (Q4): four Claude-session events (waiting for your approval, finished, failed or stopped, hit Claude's usage limit) and three inbox events (someone asked for your review, checks failed on your pull request, a Jira ticket was assigned to you). The design's Automations rows wait for 024 and its Slack row for 016.
- **Notifications live on the server**, in `cache.db` beside the inbox, so every tab and the desktop app see the same list and the same unread dots. Orynx keeps the newest 200, and none older than 30 days. Clear cache empties the list. A new server event, `notifications.changed`, makes every tab read the list again.
- **The choices on Settings › Notifications are saved on the server** with the other settings, so the browser and the desktop app share them. The one exception is the browser's own permission for desktop notifications, which each browser keeps.
- **Each notification sounds once and shows on the desktop once**, however many Orynx tabs are open: one tab plays the sound and raises the desktop notification for all of them. Every open tab shows its own pop-up.
- **"Needs you" means still waiting.** That tab and its count hold approvals Claude is still waiting on. An approval answered anywhere (here, on the Session screen, in another tab) leaves the tab, loses its buttons and stays in All.
- **Opening the panel doesn't mark anything read**, as drawn (the dots stay while it is open). Opening a notification, or pressing one of its buttons, marks that one read. Mark all read marks them all. The count on the bell and on All is the unread count; it shows 99+ above 99.
- **Allow once outside the Session screen is for commands only** (Q3), and shows the exact command, as drawn. It sends the same answer as the Session screen's Allow once, through the same server check. An edit, a push or a pull request shows Review only. Always allow and Deny stay on the Session screen.
- **The inbox notifies about what is new since its last sync.** The first sync after Orynx starts, or after you connect Jira or GitHub, records what is already there and notifies about nothing. An item that leaves the inbox and comes back is new again.
- **Pop-ups** (Q2): in the bottom-right corner, newest at the bottom, at most three at once; more wait their turn. None pops up for the session whose Session screen you're on, which already shows it inline, approval callout included (added while building, 19 Oct 2026); it's still listed under the bell and still sounds. An approval stays until you answer it, close it, or it is answered elsewhere. Any other pop-up goes after 8 seconds, and the timer stops while the pointer is over it or focus is in it. With reduced motion they appear without sliding.
- **Desktop notifications** use the browser's Notification API, which the desktop app (027) also has. Their text is the notification's title and its ticket or pull request, never a command, a path or a file's contents. Clicking one brings Orynx to the front and opens the notification's place.
- **Sounds are made in the browser with Web Audio**, as the design does: Knock, Chime and Ping, no sound files. Volume 0–100 % in steps of 5. A browser may refuse sound until the page has had a click; then the sound is skipped, never queued.
- **Defaults are the design's**: pop-ups on, desktop notifications on and only when Orynx isn't the tab you're looking at, sounds on at 60 %. Per event: approval — pop-up, desktop, Knock; finished — pop-up, desktop, Chime; failed or stopped — pop-up, desktop, Ping; usage limit — pop-up, Ping; review asked — pop-up; checks failed — pop-up; Jira assigned — nothing. Do not disturb off, quiet hours on 19:00–09:00 Monday to Friday, "Still tell me when Claude is waiting for approval" on.
- **Do not disturb and quiet hours** pause pop-ups, desktop notifications and sounds; the list and the counts keep going. "Until 9:00 tomorrow" means 09:00 local time the next day. Quiet hours that cross midnight belong to the day they start (Friday 19:00 to Saturday 09:00 is Friday's). With "Still tell me…" on, an approval still pops up, shows on the desktop and sounds.
- **Times** read "Just now", "2 min ago", "1 h ago", "Yesterday", then the date, and the list groups them under Today and Earlier by your local day.
- **The panel's gear and All settings both open Settings › Notifications**, as drawn. The design's "Simulate…" buttons are a design preview and aren't built.
- **The Settings tab goes after Skills**, as drawn: Connections, Coding agent, Skills, Notifications, Repositories, Inbox rules, Appearance, at `/settings/notifications`. Its changes save as you make them, so the page bar keeps "Changes save automatically" beside Send a test notification. Batch 028 is redesigning Settings on its own branch; whichever merges second resolves the tab list.
- **No new dependency.** The panel is Radix Popover from `radix-ui`, already in `@devhub/ui`. Notification, Web Audio and Web Locks come with the browser and Electron.
- **New `@devhub/ui` parts** (Q5), each with a story per state and a test: `Switch`, `NotificationItem` (icon, title, detail, time, unread dot, buttons), `NotificationPanel` (the popover with its head, tabs, groups and foot), `Toast` and `Slider` (a native range input). The settings page reuses `Select`, `Input` (`type="time"`), `FilterChip` (the day chips), `Checkbox`, `Pill`, `Panel` and `IconButton`.
- **Security.** Logs and server events carry notification ids and counts, never a title, a command or a ticket's text. The list is served only to the page that holds the session token, as every route is. Nothing new reaches Jira, GitHub, Slack or a repository.
- **Tests never reach the real things.** Core tests use a temporary `DEVHUB_HOME`. Browser tests fake `Notification` and `AudioContext`, the clock and the network (MSW). Browser checks fake `/api` with `page.route`.
- **People-owned files aren't edited here.** The branch hands you a script that copies the two designs into `docs/design/screens/` and adds the new parts to `docs/design/design-system.md` (Q5), and the change to `docs/product/spec.md`'s Settings section.

## Goal

I hear about the things that need me without watching Orynx: when Claude is waiting for my approval, when a session finishes or stops, and when someone asks for my review or my checks fail. Everything collects under a bell at the bottom of the sidebar, and I choose in Settings what also pops up, shows on my desktop or plays a sound, and when to be left alone.
