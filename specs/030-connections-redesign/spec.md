## Assumptions

Correct these now, or the work goes ahead on them.

- **Every word says Orynx**, never DevHub (CLAUDE.md). "DevHub reads" becomes "Orynx reads", "DevHub is using your Jira login" becomes "Orynx is using your Jira login", and so on.
- **Colours are this repo's tokens, not the design's.** The design's `tokens.css` still has the blue accent; `main` has the ink accent (18 Oct 2026). Every colour the design uses already exists as a token. Its sizes map to the type scale and its 600 weight to `font-medium`, as `docs/design-map.md` says.
- **The marks are today's brand logos** (`SourceMark`, the user's call, 17 Oct 2026), in the design's 40 px rounded tile, not its letters "J", "G" and "#".
- **Only the Connections tab changes.** The mockup's sidebar (DevHub name, New task, Automations, the bell at the top) and its tab row aren't built here: Orynx's own shell, tabs and page frame stay. The tab's body is the design's: a "Your tools" heading with one line under it, one row per tool, and the status key under the list.
- **Three states on the page.** The server's `connected` reads Connected (green check), `error` reads Needs reconnecting (amber circular arrow), `not_connected` reads Not connected (dashed plug). `needs_admin_approval`, which nothing sends yet, reads Not connected until Slack (016) needs it.
- **The row's only control is its status button**: round, 36 px, its look by state, opening the tool's dialog. Colour is never the only signal: each state has its own icon, the key spells all three out, and the button's name says it ("Jira: Connected. Manage connection"). Its tooltip is the design's: "Connected · select to manage", "Needs reconnecting · select to fix", "Not connected · select to connect".
- **The row's second line is the server's `detail`**, as today ("Personal API token for your-team.atlassian.net", or the reason it stopped working), cut with an ellipsis on one line. Not connected shows how to connect, as today.
- **The page bar says how many work**: "2 of 3 connected · 1 needs attention" on this tab, in place of "Changes save automatically" (nothing here saves on change). Under 768 px it is hidden, as the bar's text is today.
- **One dialog for every state**, from `@devhub/ui`'s `Dialog`: the mark, the name and a state pill at the top with a Close button; the state's body; the buttons at the foot. At most one primary button. Escape and Close shut it and put focus back on the row's status button.
- **What each state's dialog holds**, as 10a draws it:
  - Connected: one line ("Orynx is using your Jira login. Nothing here changes Jira itself."), a details list, and Disconnect · Cancel · Test connection · Done.
  - After Test connection: "Working. Jira answered in 180 ms." in green, Test connection becomes Test again, Cancel goes.
  - A failed test: the state turns Needs reconnecting, and the server's reason shows in a red box in the dialog.
  - Needs reconnecting: one line, an amber callout with the server's reason, the fix (Q3), a short details list, and Disconnect · Cancel · Reconnect.
  - Reconnecting: a "Checking your gh login…" line, and Reconnect turns into a disabled "Reconnecting" with a spinner (the Button's busy state). Cancel shuts the dialog; the check goes on, and the row and the page message follow its answer.
  - Not connected: the connect form (Q2), Cancel · Connect.
  - Confirm disconnect: the body becomes a red "Disconnect Jira?" alert saying what goes and what stays, and the foot becomes Keep connected · Disconnect. Focus moves to Keep connected.
- **Disconnect texts** (Orynx for DevHub, otherwise the design's): Jira "Jira tickets leave your inbox and the Tickets board, and sessions lose ticket context. Your token is removed from the keychain. Jira itself is not changed." GitHub "Pull requests leave your inbox and sessions can no longer create pull requests. Your gh login on this computer is not changed." (a saved token: "Your token is removed from the keychain."). With a session running, Jira adds "2 running sessions keep the ticket text they already have." (1: "1 running session keeps…").
- **After a change, the page says so** in a green status line under the key, as drawn: "Jira connected. Your inbox is refreshing.", "GitHub reconnected. Your inbox is refreshing.", "Jira disconnected." It stays until the next change or until you leave the tab, and replaces today's hidden announcement.
- **Test connection asks the tool now**, skipping the 2-minute reuse, and says how long it took in whole milliseconds. A new endpoint per tool returns the fresh status and the time; the status reaches every tab through `status.changed`, as today.
- **"Orynx reads" and "Orynx writes" are fixed text per tool**: Jira reads "Issues assigned to you, your sprint, comments"; GitHub reads "Pull requests, checks, review comments" and writes "Pushes session branches and opens pull requests on your click".
- **No new dependency.** Everything comes from `@devhub/ui` and what it already uses.

## Goal

Settings › Connections shows each tool's state at a glance, one round status per tool, and everything else about a connection lives in one dialog: what Orynx can see, a test that it still works, the fix when it stopped working, and a disconnect that says what you lose before it happens.
